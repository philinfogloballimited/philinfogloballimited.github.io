What we do.

Honest recruitment — legal, safe and reliable.

Our mission is to be customer focused in all areas of our operations and to provide excellent, dependable and reliable workers to clients in both the public and private sector. Our vision is to be recognised as a recruitment leader — providing the best quality workers in the country and beyond.

We are fully registered and licensed by the National Employment Authority (NEA), the Kenyan government body that governs recruitment. That means every placement we make is documented, lawful and accountable.
