Partner With PHILINFO GLOBAL LIMITED

We supply vetted, work-ready staff from Kenya to employers in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Dubai, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain and Europe — and to companies hiring locally. Tell us what you need and a consultant will come back with a sourcing plan, timelines and a fee proposal.

Start a partnership request +254 724 475 843

  • Fast turnaround

    Registered, verified portfolios on file mean shortlists in days, not months — plus fresh applicants from our current advertising.

  • Documents handled

    Passports, medicals, good conduct, work permits and visas are processed in-house, so you receive candidates who are actually cleared to travel.

  • Screened candidates

    Interviewed against your brief, references checked and medical fitness confirmed before anyone reaches your selection panel.

  • NEA licensed

    Fully registered and licensed by the National Employment Authority (NEA) — a compliant, documented process that stands up to scrutiny.

  • Local and foreign

    One partner for overseas deployments and for Kenyan hires, with offices in Nairobi, Mombasa and Eldoret.

  • One point of contact

    A named consultant owns your file, follows up after arrival and stays reachable for the whole contract.

How a partnership works

  1. 01

    Brief

    Roles, numbers, salary bands, age limits and start dates — agreed in writing.

  2. 02

    Source & screen

    We advertise, search our database, interview and verify documents.

  3. 03

    Shortlist & interview

    CVs, certificates and permits submitted in your format; interviews arranged.

  4. 04

    Travel & follow-up

    Permits, visas, tickets and arrival support — then check-ins after start.

  • Caregivers, nurses, nurse assistants

Commercial terms

  • Placement fees quoted per role or per candidate, agreed in writing before we start.
  • Volume and mass-recruitment campaigns priced separately.
  • Replacement terms stated on the contract — no verbal promises.
  • Candidates are never charged for placement.
