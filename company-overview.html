Company Overview - PHILINFO GLOBAL LIMITED

  1. Home
  3. Company Overview

We are a recruitment agency located in Nairobi, Kenya, with branches in Mombasa and Eldoret, founded in the year 2011. We have become one of the most sought-after recruitment agencies by both job seekers and employers within and outside the Republic of Kenya.

We are fully registered and licensed by the National Employment Authority (NEA) — the Kenyan national government body that governs the Kenyan labour sector in recruitment processes — and we are one of the fastest growing recruitment agencies in Kenya.

Our mission

To be customer focused in all areas of our operations and provide excellent, dependable and reliable workers to our clientele, both in the public and private sector.

Our vision

To become recognised as one of the recruitment company leaders in providing the best quality workers in the country and beyond.

Services for foreign recruitment

  • Job placements — verified employers in the Gulf and Europe.
  • Advertising — vacancies promoted through our offices and networks.
  • Training and development — pre-departure and skills briefings.
  • Resume writing — CVs in the format overseas employers expect.
  • Advisory and consultation — honest guidance on destinations and contracts.
  • Passport processing — new passports and waiting cards.
  • Work permit processing — prepared, submitted and tracked.
  • Work visa processing — with medicals and good conduct coordinated.
  • Visitors visa processing — short visits, family trips and business travel.
  • Foreign jobs consultation — one-to-one sessions before you commit.
  • Foreign tours visa booking — travel booking support.

Countries we recruit for

Saudi Arabia, Dubai, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Europe.

Who we provide for clientele

  • Education: teachers, tutors
  • Health: caregivers, nurses, nurse assistants
  • Hospitality: waiters, waitresses, baristas, chefs, housemaids, stewards, cleaners
  • Transport & logistics: drivers, riders, forklift operators
  • Construction: panel beaters, masons, ductmen, construction helpers, electricians
