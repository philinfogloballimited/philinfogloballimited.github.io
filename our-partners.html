Regulators & official platforms

We are licensed and registered in Kenya. You can verify us directly with the authority that regulates recruitment agencies — always do this before paying anyone for a job abroad.

Regulator

National Employment Authority (NEA)

The Kenyan state body that licenses private employment agencies and oversees foreign recruitment. PHILINFO GLOBAL LIMITED is fully registered and licensed by NEA.

nea.go.ke
Official platform

NEAIMS

The National Employment Authority Integrated Management System — the government portal where job seekers register, employers post vacancies and agencies like ours are listed.

neaims.go.ke
